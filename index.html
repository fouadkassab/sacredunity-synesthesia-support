Synesthesia / Support

Keep your creativity flowing.

Manifest your sound into light and movement. Bring your vision into reality.

For help with Synesthesia, contact me@fooji.life. Include your device model, iOS version, app version and a short description of what happened. A screenshot can help, but please remove private information before sending it.

Sound and microphone

Tap Begin to start sound sensing and allow microphone access when asked. If access was denied, enable it for Synesthesia in iOS Settings. If sound stops responding after an interruption, pause and restart sensing. Close and reopen the app if needed.

Camera and photographs

The live camera backdrop is optional. Enable it under Backdrop and grant camera permission. You can also select background photographs or import your own. Camera and microphone permissions can be changed in iOS Settings.

Recording and monitoring

Use the record control to capture the visuals and microphone audio, then stop to save the video to Photos. Allow permission to add videos and keep enough free storage available. Use headphones or a supported audio interface for live monitoring. Speaker monitoring is blocked to reduce feedback.

Full-screen and projection

Use immersion mode to hide the controls. Double-tap to bring them back. To project the visuals, open iOS Control Center, choose Screen Mirroring and select a compatible receiver. Mirroring includes whatever is visible on the device screen.

Performance and comfort

For long sessions, lower visual richness or turn off the camera if the device becomes warm or motion slows. Take a break if moving light or flashing patterns cause discomfort. Synesthesia is a creative tool, not a medical or diagnostic product.
