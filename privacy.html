Synesthesia / Privacy

Your sound. Your space.

Effective 7 September 2026. This policy covers Synesthesia for iPhone and iPad and this support website. Synesthesia is presented under the Sacred Unity brand and published by QUIRKY COOKING PTY LTD. Contact: me@fooji.life.

On-device creative processing

Synesthesia processes microphone sound and optional camera imagery on your device to create audiovisual effects. The app does not send your microphone audio, camera frames, imported photographs or recordings to us. It has no account registration, advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK.

Microphone and camera

Microphone access is used for sound-responsive visuals, recording and optional headphone monitoring. Camera access is optional and is used for a live backdrop or hand interaction. Hand analysis is used for interaction, not to identify you. You control these permissions through iOS Settings. If you record with a live camera backdrop enabled, that imagery becomes part of the exported video.

Photographs and recordings

You choose personal images through the system photo picker. Selected images are copied into the app's local storage for use as backgrounds. Recording temporarily stores video and audio on the device while preparing an export. The app requests permission to add the finished recording to Photos, and removes temporary files after a successful export. Interrupted or failed exports may leave temporary files until removed by the app's deletion or the operating system.

Videos saved to Photos remain there until you delete them. Your own iCloud Photos, device backup and sharing settings may cause Apple or another service you choose to store or transmit your content. Those services operate under their own privacy terms. Uninstalling the app does not delete videos already saved to Photos.

Settings and local storage

Your tuning preferences and selected background choices are stored locally so they can be restored. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to device backup and restore behaviour. You can revoke microphone and camera permissions without contacting us.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and troubleshoot the issue. Email is handled by the email services used to deliver and store that correspondence. Please avoid sending sensitive recordings or personal information that is not needed for support. You may request access, correction or deletion of your support correspondence by contacting us. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or to resolve an ongoing issue.

This website and external links

This website uses GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising cookies or tracking pixels. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Links to Apple, Unsplash or other websites take you to services governed by their own policies. Apple may process purchase, download and diagnostic information according to your Apple settings and its privacy policy.

Updates and questions

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. For privacy questions or concerns, email me@fooji.life.
